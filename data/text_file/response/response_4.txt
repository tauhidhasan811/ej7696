## API 510 - Pressure Vessel Inspector: Exam Preparation Content

This document outlines the essential knowledge areas for the API 510 Pressure Vessel Inspector certification examination. It is structured according to the API 510 Body of Knowledge and references the specified codes and standards from the Effectivity Sheet. Candidates are expected to possess a broad knowledge base relating to the maintenance, inspection, repair, and alteration of pressure vessels.

---

### I. THICKNESS MEASUREMENTS, INSPECTION INTERVALS AND VESSEL INTEGRITY

This section covers the fundamental principles and calculations necessary for assessing the integrity of pressure vessels, determining inspection intervals, and evaluating various design and operational parameters.

**Key Topics:**

*   **Corrosion Rates and Inspection Intervals:** Understanding how to calculate corrosion rates and establish appropriate inspection intervals to ensure continued safe operation of pressure vessels.
    *   *Referenced Documents:* API 510, Pressure Vessel Inspection Code.
*   **Joint Efficiencies:** Application of joint efficiency factors in design and remaining life calculations, particularly for welded joints.
    *   *Referenced Documents:* ASME Section VIII, Division 1 (UG, UW).
*   **Static Head:** Calculation and consideration of static head pressure in vessel design, operation, and pressure testing.
    *   *Referenced Documents:* ASME Section VIII, Division 1.
*   **Internal Pressure:** Principles and calculations related to internal pressure design and stress analysis for pressure vessels.
    *   *Referenced Documents:* ASME Section VIII, Division 1 (UG).
*   **External Pressure:** Principles and calculations related to external pressure design and stability analysis for pressure vessels.
    *   *Referenced Documents:* ASME Section VIII, Division 1 (UG).
*   **Pressure Testing:** Requirements and procedures for conducting pressure tests (hydrostatic, pneumatic) on pressure vessels, including safety precautions and acceptance criteria.
    *   *Referenced Documents:* API 510, Pressure Vessel Inspection Code; ASME Section VIII, Division 1 (UG).
*   **Impact Testing:** Criteria and requirements for impact testing of materials to ensure adequate toughness, especially at low temperatures.
    *   *Referenced Documents:* ASME Section VIII, Division 1 (UCS).
*   **Weld Size for Attachment Welds at Openings:** Determination of appropriate weld sizes for reinforcing pads and other attachments at vessel openings.
    *   *Referenced Documents:* ASME Section VIII, Division 1 (UW, Appendices).
*   **Nozzle Reinforcement:** Principles and calculations for reinforcing nozzles and other openings in pressure vessels to maintain structural integrity.
    *   *Referenced Documents:* ASME Section VIII, Division 1 (UG, Appendices 1-4).

---

### II. WELDING PROCEDURE AND QUALIFICATION EVALUATION

This section focuses on the evaluation of welding procedures, welder qualifications, and general welding requirements as specified by ASME and API standards.

**Key Topics:**

*   **Weld Procedure Review (WPS, PQR, WPQ):** Understanding the content and requirements for Welding Procedure Specifications (WPS), Procedure Qualification Records (PQR), and Welder Performance Qualifications (WPQ). This includes verifying essential variables, supplementary essential variables, and nonessential variables.
    *   *Referenced Documents:* ASME Section IX, Qualification Standard for Welding, Brazing, and Fusing Procedures (Welding only).
*   **General Welding Requirements:** Knowledge of general welding requirements applicable to pressure vessel construction, repair, and alteration.
    *   *Referenced Documents:* ASME Section VIII, Division 1 (UW); API 510, Pressure Vessel Inspection Code.
*   **Welding Processes:** Focus on the following specific welding processes:
    *   Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW)
    *   Gas Tungsten Arc Welding (GTAW)
    *   Gas Metal Arc Welding (GMAW)
    *   Submerged Arc Welding (SAW)
*   **Base Metals:** Focus on base metals categorized by P-Numbers:
    *   P-No. 1
    *   P-No. 3
    *   P-No. 4
    *   P-No. 5
    *   P-No. 8

### III. NONDESTRUCTIVE EXAMINATION

This section covers the principles, application, and interpretation of various Nondestructive Examination (NDE) methods used for inspecting pressure vessels, based on ASME Section V.

**Key Topics:**

*   **Article 1 (General):** General requirements for NDE, including responsibilities, personnel qualifications, procedures, and record-keeping.
    *   *Referenced Documents:* ASME Section V, Nondestructive Examination, Article 1.
*   **Article 2 (Radiography):** Principles, techniques, equipment, safety, and interpretation of radiographic examination for detecting internal discontinuities.
    *   *Referenced Documents:* ASME Section V, Nondestructive Examination, Article 2.
*   **Article 6 (Liquid Penetrant):** Principles, techniques, materials, and interpretation of liquid penetrant examination for detecting surface-breaking discontinuities.
    *   *Referenced Documents:* ASME Section V, Nondestructive Examination, Article 6.
*   **Article 7 (Magnetic Particle - Yoke and Prod only):** Principles, techniques (specifically yoke and prod methods), equipment, and interpretation of magnetic particle examination for detecting surface and near-surface discontinuities in ferromagnetic materials.
    *   *Referenced Documents:* ASME Section V, Nondestructive Examination, Article 7.
*   **Article 23 (Ultrasonic Thickness - SE-797 only):** Principles and application of ultrasonic examination specifically for thickness measurement, as detailed in Section SE-797.
    *   *Referenced Documents:* ASME Section V, Nondestructive Examination, Article 23 (Section SE-797 only).

### IV. PRACTICAL KNOWLEDGE - GENERAL & SPECIFIC

This section encompasses a broad range of practical knowledge derived from various API Recommended Practices and ASME standards, covering damage mechanisms, inspection practices, pressure-relieving devices, material verification, and repair procedures.

**Key Topics:**

*   **API 510 Requirements:** Comprehensive understanding of the in-service inspection, rating, repair, and alteration requirements for pressure vessels.
    *   *Referenced Documents:* API 510, Pressure Vessel Inspection Code.
*   **API RP 571 - Damage Mechanisms:** Knowledge of specific damage mechanisms affecting fixed equipment in the refining industry.
    *   *Referenced Documents:* API Recommended Practice 571, Damage Mechanisms Affecting Fixed Equipment in the Refining Industry (Sections: 2, 3.3, 3.8, 3.9, 3.11, 3.14, 3.15, 3.17, 3.22, 3.27, 3.35, 3.36, 3.37, 3.46, 3.61, 3.67).
*   **API RP 572 - Inspection Practices:** Practical knowledge of inspection practices specifically for pressure vessels.
    *   *Referenced Documents:* API Recommended Practice 572, Inspection Practices for Pressure Vessels.
*   **API RP 576 - Inspection of Pressure-relieving Devices:** Understanding the inspection requirements and practices for pressure-relieving devices.
    *   *Referenced Documents:* API Recommended Practice 576, Inspection of Pressure-relieving Devices.
*   **API RP 577 - Welding Processes, Inspection, and Metallurgy:** Practical knowledge related to welding processes, inspection techniques, and metallurgical considerations.
    *   *Referenced Documents:* API Recommended Practice 577, Welding Processes, Inspection, and Metallurgy.
*   **API RP 578 - Material Verification Program:** Understanding the requirements for a material verification program for new and existing assets.
    *   *Referenced Documents:* API Recommended Practice 578, Material Verification Program for New and Existing Assets.
*   **ASME PCC-2 - Repair of Pressure Equipment and Piping:** Knowledge of specific articles related to the repair of pressure equipment and piping.
    *   *Referenced Documents:* ASME PCC-2, Repair of Pressure Equipment and Piping (Articles: 101, 201, 202, 209, 210, 211, 212, 215, 216, 304, 305, 312, 501, 502).
